## Overview

When a production system fails, the evidence is spread across thousands of log lines from many
components. sysCrow is an autonomous system that detects anomalous behavior in logs and points to
the components and events most likely related to the fault. It adapts ideas from peer-reviewed
research into a practical, end-to-end tool.

## Approach

sysCrow combines two complementary methods.

**Supervised anomaly detection.** Inspired by DeepLog, a sequence model learns the normal patterns
of log events and flags deviations from them as anomalies.

**Fault localization with unsupervised segmentation.** An unsupervised method segments logs into
meaningful contexts, which highlights the suspicious components and events around a fault.
